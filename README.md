# AgriMarket Platform

> Part of the [worlds-biggest-software-project](https://github.com/worlds-biggest-software-project) initiative.
>
> An AI-native, open-source agricultural marketplace for commodity trading, real-time price discovery, and direct farmer-buyer connections.

AgriMarket Platform is a digital marketplace that connects crop producers, agri-buyers, traders, and lenders in a single transparent venue. It targets the gap between derivatives-focused exchanges, geographically concentrated regional marketplaces, and produce-only platforms by offering a unified, AI-driven environment for spot trading, quality verification, and embedded trade finance.

---

## Why AgriMarket Platform?

- Benchmark exchanges like CME Group are derivatives-focused and do not serve direct farmer-to-buyer trade.
- Leading commercial marketplaces (agribazaar, FBN) are geographically concentrated in single regions such as India or North America.
- Newer platforms (AGRA Marketplace, Agridex, Agrimp) struggle with liquidity and limited international scale.
- Produce Pay's embedded-finance differentiator is restricted to fresh produce, leaving grains and pulses underserved.
- Public systems like AMIS provide authoritative data but are not transactional, leaving a gap for an open, AI-native marketplace that combines transparency with execution.

## AI-Native Advantage

AI sits at the centre of the platform: a dynamic pricing engine adjusts offers in real time using local supply forecasts, weather-driven crop stress data, logistics availability, and buyer bid depth. An AI match engine pairs supply listings to buyer demand profiles based on quality specs, logistics proximity, and historical trade reliability. AI also automates short-term trade finance underwriting from production and transaction history, and flags fraud or quality disputes by analysing grading certificates, sensor data, and counterparty behaviour.

---

## Tech Stack & Deployment

The platform is expected to align with established agricultural and trade standards: GIPSA grain grading, Codex Alimentarius food quality specifications, GS1 traceability identifiers, AMIS market-information data standards, and SWIFT / ISO 20022 messaging for cross-border settlement. Deployment, SDK, and integration details remain to be specified.

## Market Context

Over 60% of agricultural product transactions are projected to occur on digital marketplaces by 2026, embedded within a global agricultural commodity trade estimated at trillions of dollars annually (Farmonaut, 2026). Incumbent pricing is dominated by transaction or commission models in the 1–3% range, with FBN combining membership and transaction fees. Primary buyers include smallholder and mid-scale producers, mills, processors, exporters, traders, agri-lenders, and government agencies seeking market transparency.
